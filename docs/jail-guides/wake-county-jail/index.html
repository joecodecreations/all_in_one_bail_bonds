How to bail someone out of the Wake County jail

A step-by-step guide for families: how to confirm someone is in the Wake County Detention Center, find the bond amount and get the bond posted.

Call (919) 247-9397Text us

Please note: All In One Bail Bonds is a private bail bond agency. We are not affiliated with any sheriff's office, jail, court or government agency. Addresses and phone numbers on this page come from official county and North Carolina Judicial Branch websites; call the facility to confirm before you drive there.

Wake County uses two facilities: the Wake County Detention Center at 3301 Hammond Road, and the John H. Baker Jr. Public Safety Center at 330 S Salisbury St in downtown Raleigh. Ask which one the person is in before you drive.

Step 1: Confirm where they are

Wake County arrest records search (recent arrests). You can also call 919-773-7930. Have the person's full legal name and date of birth ready.

Step 2: Find out the bond and the conditions

A magistrate sets release conditions soon after most arrests. Domestic violence charges and certain serious felonies must wait for a judge, which can take up to 48 hours. If the condition is a secured bond, a bondsman can post it.

Step 3: Call a bondsman

Call All In One Bail Bonds at (919) 247-9397. We confirm the bond, tell you the premium (never more than 15% of the bond in North Carolina) and explain what the cosigner signs.

Step 4: Paperwork and payment

Call first and we will tell you what can be done by phone or electronically and what needs a signature in person at our Raleigh office. We accept some payment arrangements.

Step 5: We post the bond

A licensed agent posts the bond with the jail. The jail then processes the release on its own schedule.

Step 6: Court

The Wake County Justice Center is at 300 S. Salisbury St, Raleigh. Check the date on the NC eCourts portal or call the Clerk of Superior Court at (919) 792-4000. Go to every court date.

Wake County jail questions

What is the address of the Wake County jail?

The Wake County Detention Center is at 3301 Hammond Road, Raleigh, NC 27610. The John H. Baker Jr. Public Safety Center is at 330 S Salisbury St, Raleigh, NC 27601.

How do I find out if someone is in the Wake County jail?

Use the Wake County arrest records search (recent arrests), or call 919-773-7930. You can also call us at (919) 247-9397 and we will check.

How long does release take after the bond is posted?

That is up to the jail. Release processing varies with how busy the facility is. No bondsman can promise a release time.

How much does a bail bond cost in North Carolina?

North Carolina law caps the bail bond premium at 15% of the bond amount (N.C. Gen. Stat. § 58-71-95). On a $5,000 bond the premium can be no more than $750. A bondsman may also ask for collateral, but may not charge extra application or processing fees. Call us for the exact cost of a specific bond.

Need someone out of jail tonight?

A licensed bail agent answers 24 hours a day. Have the person's full name and the county ready if you can.
