How it works

  1. Call us with the basics. Tell us the person's full name, date of birth if you know it, and which county jail they are in. We find the bond amount and the conditions the magistrate or judge set.
  2. We explain the cost and the paperwork. You hear the premium and any collateral up front, before you sign anything. The person who signs with us (the cosigner) agrees to make sure the defendant goes to every court date.
  3. We post the bond at the jail. A licensed agent posts the bond with the jail. The jail then processes the release. How long that takes is up to the jail, not the bondsman.

Raleigh bail bond questions

Where is your Raleigh office?

6104 Westgate Road, Suite 110, Raleigh, NC 27617. Near Brier Creek and RDU, off Glenwood Avenue (US-70). Call (919) 247-9397 before you come so an agent can meet you.

Which jail serves Wake County?

People arrested in Wake County are usually held at the Wake County Detention Center, 3301 Hammond Road, Raleigh. See our Wake County page for phone numbers and the official lookup.

Do I have to come to the Raleigh office in person?

Not always. Call first. We will tell you what can be handled by phone or electronically for your bond and what needs a signature in person.

How much does a bail bond cost in North Carolina?

North Carolina law caps the bail bond premium at 15% of the bond amount (N.C. Gen. Stat. § 58-71-95). On a $5,000 bond the premium can be no more than $750. A bondsman may also ask for collateral, but may not charge extra application or processing fees. Call us for the exact cost of a specific bond.

Can I make payments?

We accept some payment arrangements. Whether a payment arrangement is available depends on the bond and your situation. When part of the premium is paid after release, North Carolina law requires a written agreement, signed by both sides, that lists every payment date and amount, and you get a copy.
