Bail bondsman for Greensboro and Guilford County

We post misdemeanor, felony and DWI bonds for people held at the Greensboro Detention Center. Guilford County is served from our Durham office, and you can reach an agent at any hour.

Towns we serve in Guilford County include Greensboro, High Point, Jamestown, Gibsonville, Summerfield, Oak Ridge, Pleasant Garden, Stokesdale.

How to bail someone out in Guilford County

  1. Confirm where they are. Check the official lookup or call the jail at (336) 641-2700. Or call us and we will find out.
  2. Find out the bond. A magistrate usually sets release conditions soon after arrest. Some charges must wait for a judge, which can take up to 48 hours.
  3. Call (919) 247-9397. We tell you the premium and any collateral before you sign anything.
  4. We post the bond. A licensed agent posts it with the jail, and the jail processes the release.

What it costs

North Carolina law caps the bail bond premium at 15% of the bond amount. We accept some payment arrangements. Use the cost calculator or read about payment arrangements.

Guilford County bail questions

Where is the Guilford County jail?

People arrested in Guilford County are generally held at the Greensboro Detention Center, 201 S Edgeworth St., Greensboro. Guilford County also uses the High Point Detention Center, 507 E. Green Drive, High Point. Call the facility to confirm someone is there before you drive over.

How do I find out if someone is in the Guilford County jail?

Use the Guilford County inmate lookup (official) or call the jail at (336) 641-2700. You can also call us at (919) 247-9397 and we will check for you.

Where is criminal court in Guilford County?

The Guilford County Courthouse is at 201 South Eugene St, Greensboro, NC 27401. The Clerk of Superior Court's number is (336) 412-7300. You can look up a court date on the NC eCourts portal.

How much is a bail bond in Guilford County?

The same rule applies in every North Carolina county: the premium cannot be more than 15% of the bond amount. On a $2,500 bond that is no more than $375. Call us for the exact cost of a specific bond.
