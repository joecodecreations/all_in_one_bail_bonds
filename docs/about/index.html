What we do

All In One Bail Bonds provides fast, affordable and discreet bail bond service in Raleigh and throughout Wake County, Durham County, Wilson County and the surrounding counties, and writes bonds across North Carolina. Our agents are available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, so people can go back to their jobs and families while their case moves through court.

How we work

  • We answer. Day, night, weekend or holiday.
  • We explain before you sign. You hear the premium and any collateral up front.
  • We follow the rules. North Carolina caps the premium at 15%, and every payment arrangement goes in writing with a copy for you.
  • We keep it discreet. Your family's business stays your family's business.
