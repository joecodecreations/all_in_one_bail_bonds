Missed court? Bonds after a failure to appear

A missed court date does not fix itself. The sooner it is dealt with, the more options there are.

Call (919) 247-9397Text us

What happens when court is missed

The judge can issue an order for arrest and begin forfeiting the bond that was posted. The cosigner on that bond can end up responsible for the full amount.

If we wrote your bond

Call us right away, even if the date was missed by accident. Do not wait to be picked up. We will tell you where things stand and what needs to happen next.

If you need a new bond

After an arrest on an order for arrest, new release conditions are set, and courts generally set a higher secured bond after a missed date. Call us with the county and we will find out the amount.

What it costs

North Carolina caps the bail bond premium at 15% of the bond amount. We accept some payment arrangements. See the cost calculator.

Have this ready when you call

  1. The person's full legal name (and date of birth if you have it)
  2. The county or jail where they are being held
  3. The charge and the bond amount, if you know them
  4. Your own name and a phone number where we can reach you

Questions

How do I check my court date?

Use the NC eCourts portal or call the Clerk of Superior Court in the county where the case is. Our county pages list each clerk's phone number.

Can I just turn myself in?

People do, and having a bondsman lined up beforehand can shorten the time in custody. Call us first so we can explain how it would work in your county. For advice about the case itself, talk to a licensed attorney.

What does the person who signs the bond agree to?

The cosigner (also called the indemnitor) promises that the defendant will go to every court date. If the defendant misses court and the bond is forfeited, the cosigner can be responsible for the full bond amount.
