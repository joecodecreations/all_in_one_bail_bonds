Bail bondsman for Sanford and Lee County

We post misdemeanor, felony and DWI bonds for people held at the Lee County Detention Facility. Lee County is served from our Raleigh office, and you can reach an agent at any hour.

Towns we serve in Lee County include Sanford, Broadway.

How to bail someone out in Lee County

  1. Confirm where they are. Check the official lookup or call the jail at 919-718-4567. Or call us and we will find out.
  2. Find out the bond. A magistrate usually sets release conditions soon after arrest. Some charges must wait for a judge, which can take up to 48 hours.
  3. Call (919) 247-9397. We tell you the premium and any collateral before you sign anything.
  4. We post the bond. A licensed agent posts it with the jail, and the jail processes the release.

What it costs

North Carolina law caps the bail bond premium at 15% of the bond amount. We accept some payment arrangements. Use the cost calculator or read about payment arrangements.

Lee County bail questions

Where is the Lee County jail?

People arrested in Lee County are generally held at the Lee County Detention Facility, 1401 Elm Street, Sanford. Call the facility to confirm someone is there before you drive over.

How do I find out if someone is in the Lee County jail?

Use the Lee County inmate lookup (official) or call the jail at 919-718-4567. You can also call us at (919) 247-9397 and we will check for you.

Where is criminal court in Lee County?

The Lee County Courthouse is at 1400 S Horner Blvd, Sanford, NC 27330. The Clerk of Superior Court's number is (919) 718-6300. You can look up a court date on the NC eCourts portal.

How much is a bail bond in Lee County?

The same rule applies in every North Carolina county: the premium cannot be more than 15% of the bond amount. On a $2,500 bond that is no more than $375. Call us for the exact cost of a specific bond.
